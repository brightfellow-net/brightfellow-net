---
lede: An app for preparing the weekly order of service together, from first draft, through review, to the bulletin and slides.
note: Currently being piloted with a church in Jakarta.
problem:
  - In many churches, the weekly liturgy is put together in Word files passed around in group chats. It’s often unclear which version is the latest, corrections are scattered across messages, and then everything is copied again into the bulletin and the slides.
  - This work matters, and it shouldn’t be that hard.
features:
  - Draft the order of service together as a team, in one place.
  - Review it together, so everyone sees the same version.
  - Produce the bulletin and slides from that same order of service.
selfHost: Like every Brightfellow tool, it can be run for free on your church’s own server.
hosted: Don’t want to look after a server? We can run it for your church, with a separate space for each church. Pricing will be announced.
invite:
  title: Want to try it early?
  text: We’re looking for a few churches willing to use the app early and share feedback. Your feedback helps shape what it becomes.
